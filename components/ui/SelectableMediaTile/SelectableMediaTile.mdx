# SelectableMediaTile

<ComponentLinks slug="selectable-media-tile" />

A vertical, image-first toggle: a full-bleed image on top, a caption below, and a persistent selected state, with the whole tile as a single `<button>` click target. Use it for image or avatar grids where each cell toggles on and off — a hero-photo picker, a gallery multi-select, an avatar chooser.

## Default

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

`selected`, `disabled`, `caption`, and `aspectRatio` are all Controls on this story — toggle them in the Controls panel.

## Patterns

The irreducible composition is a single-select image grid — one tile pinned at a time, clicking the pinned tile clears it. Selection shows a brand ring, a check overlay on the image, and an emphasised caption, which Controls can't express.
