# SelectableMediaTile

<ComponentLinks slug="selectable-media-tile" />

A vertical, image-first toggle: a full-bleed image on top, a caption below, and a persistent selected state, with the whole tile as a single `<button aria-pressed>` click target when it stands alone, or a native radio / checkbox when it sits inside a group (see Patterns). Use it for image or avatar grids where each cell toggles on and off — a hero-photo picker, a gallery multi-select, an avatar chooser.

`selected`, `disabled`, `caption`, and `aspectRatio` are all Controls on this story — toggle them in the Controls panel.

## Patterns

The irreducible composition is a single-select image grid — one tile pinned at a time, clicking the pinned tile clears it. Selection shows a brand ring, a check overlay on the image, and an emphasised caption, which Controls can't express.

<Canvas of={Stories.SingleSelectGrid} />

### Pick one

Selection semantics follow cardinality (ADR-045). A **single** stand-alone tile is a toggle. When tiles are a **pick-one** set, put them inside a [`RadioGroup`](/?path=/docs/components-radio-group--docs) and give each a `value`: the tile renders as a `<label>` around a visually hidden native radio, the group is one Tab stop, the arrow keys move focus and selection together, and assistive tech announces "radio, checked". Selection comes from the group — drop `selected` and `onClick`. For pick-many, use a `CheckboxGroup` the same way.
