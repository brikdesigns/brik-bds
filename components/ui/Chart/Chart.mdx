# Chart

<ComponentLinks slug="chart" name="Chart" />

The data-visualization family (ADR-050). `BarChart`, `LineChart` and `DonutChart` render inside one `ChartFrame` — title, legend, axis labels, tooltip and a "Show data table" toggle. Loading, empty and error states come from the DataView shell. `Sparkline` is the frameless trend for a `Stat`'s `supplementary` slot.

Colour comes only from the 11 chart colours: bars, dots, slices and swatches fill with `--bds-background-chart-*`, lines stroke with `--bds-border-chart-*`, each named directly so DevTools shows the token. Pick one per series with `ChartSeries.color` (type `ChartColor`). Peer series default to `CHART_CATEGORICAL_COLORS` in order; a single series is `categorical-1`.

**The chart-type rule.** Pick by the question asked:

| Question | Chart |
|---|---|
| How do categories compare? | **Column** (`BarChart`), the default. `orientation="horizontal"` for long labels. |
| What is each bar made of? | **Stacked column** (`BarChart` with `stacked`), segments sum to the category total. Keep it to 4 or 5 series. |
| How did it change over continuous time? | **Line** (`LineChart`). |
| What share of the whole? | **Donut** (`DonutChart`), only at 5 categories or fewer. With 6 or more, use column. |
| Which way is it trending, next to a number? | **Sparkline** inside `Stat`. |
| Running total over time? | Area is not in v1. Render it as a line. |

### Grouped series

More than one series shows a legend. Each legend item toggles its series; the last visible series cannot be hidden.

<Canvas of={Stories.GroupedSeries} />

### Stacked series

`stacked` draws one bar per category, its segments summing to the total, in both orientations. The legend re-stacks the visible series. The data table adds a Total column.

### Sparkline in a Stat

The Stat's value and trend are the text; the sparkline adds the shape. Its `label` names the image and captions a visually hidden table of every value.
