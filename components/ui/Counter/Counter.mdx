## Notes

- `status` and `size` are pure color/dimension axes driven by Controls. Tone semantics: `positive` completed items / `negative` alerts / `warning` pending / `neutral` inactive / `info` in-flight. `emphasis="brand"` is the hue source for general counts and notifications.
