# App bar

<ComponentLinks slug="app-bar" />

The product app-shell bar: breadcrumbs on the left, then `actions` and a `utilities` cluster on the right. `TopNavigation` is the marketing navbar; `AppBar` is the product app-shell bar.

## Notes

> **Why a `<div>`, not a `<header>`.** The product-shell canon forbids a banner landmark outside `<main>` (page-structure, "Product app shell"), so the bar adds no landmark of its own.

> **The shell owns pinning.** Pass `<AppBar />` as `AppShell`'s `topBar`; the shell wrapper sets `position: sticky`, the z-index and the height. The bar sets none of these and fills the slot.
