5. How This Was Measured

  • The 140 names: rule 9 of tokens/naming-canon-baseline.json on main at be4798fc.
  • Figma-backed: declared in tokens/figma-tokens*.css, tokens/modes-*.css or tokens/layout-fluid.css (432 names), or a variable in design-tokens/brand-kits/brik.json. Every --bds-* replacement in section 3 was checked against that set, except the six that wait on a promote.
  • Consumer counts are occurrences, not files. BDS: main outside tokens/, dist/, design-tokens/, docs/, scripts/, tests/ and *.md. Portal: src/ on staging. Site: brikdesigns src/ on main.
  • Token Files counts var() references under tokens/, including the opt-in tokens/compat/bridge.css and tokens/motion-classes.css. A delete must move those too.
  • Value check: each alias and its replacement were resolved in all 25 selectors that declare tokens (root, dark, .theme-brand-brik, every [data-mode-*]). Declarations inside @media were not compared.
  • Not scanned: client sites under web/*, brik-templates, brik-website-themes. A zero here is a zero in three repos, not in the fleet.
  • Figma variable types (Color, Number, String, Boolean, Timing, Easing) and what each binds to: Figma, Overview of variables, collections, and modes, fetched 2026-11-18. That page says a number is "whole numbers or any decimal number up to the hundredth place" and letter spacing is "interpreted as Px, not %". It is silent on ratios, clamp() and currentColor; those keep verdicts rest on the number and colour definitions, not on a stated limit.

6. Unverified

  • Whether the Style Dictionary sync reads Figma's Timing and Easing types today. The eight motion names in group D depend on it. Figma's six duration-* names already sync; no easing does.
  • Whether --bds-shadow-overlay can arrive the way --bds-shadow-sm … -xl do. Figma's page says shadow colour and offsets bind to variables; it does not say a whole effect does.
  • --bds-tooltip-background equals --bds-background-inverse in light mode. Dark mode was not compared, so it is on the promote list, not the delete list.
