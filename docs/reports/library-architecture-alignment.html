Assessment only. No token, Figma or component change was made. Written during the session on #3279 (umbrella #3239).

We are not aligned because the written system still teaches the old model in several places, and is silent on the three points this session got wrong. Confidence: HIGH on the evidence below; the model in section 1 is my reading and needs your yes.

  4. CASCADE.md still permits manual overrides in five lines, next to the line that forbids them.
  5. Figma moved ahead of code, docs and tickets on 2026-11-19. Figma has plain families. Code, the grammar file, four doc pages and #3279's acceptance criteria still say color/system/* and color/accent/*.

5. Topic 04 — why an agent proposed deleting brand colours

What I did: you asked for seven plain families in Foundations. Five already exist by name in the Brand Kit. I treated two ramps with one name as duplication and proposed removing the Brand Kit's.

  1. No written rule for the overlap. The architecture page never says the two libraries can share a family name, or which side yields. Until 2026-11-19 they could not collide, because Foundations used color/system/*.
  2. The architecture page hides the brand families. "poppy / tan / etc." does not tell a reader that blue, green, orange, purple, yellow and pink are Brik brand colours with brand values.
  3. I stretched this Project's goal. #3239 is "a name is born in one place". I applied it as "a name has one definition". Those are different, and theming depends on the second being false.
  4. The grammar file lists families with no owner. naming-grammar.json:37 is one flat list, so nothing marks a family as brand property.
  5. I did not apply a rule that already exists. Brand colour is client-visible identity, and the operating manual says an agent never originates a client-visible cut. That alone should have stopped the suggestion.

Fix: one "Ownership and overlap" section on the architecture page carrying section 1 of this report, with the full list of brand families, and one line in brik-bds/CLAUDE.md pointing at it: never propose removing or re-valuing a Brand Kit colour.
