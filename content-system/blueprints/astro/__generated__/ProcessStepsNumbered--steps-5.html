Our process

How it works

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    We learn your goals, timeline and constraints before anything is proposed.

  2. 2

    Scope and plan

    You get a written scope with milestones, owners and a clear price.

  3. 3

    Build and review

    We deliver in stages and check each one with you before moving on.

  4. 4

    Launch and support

    We go live together and stay on hand for the first weeks after.

  5. 5

    Ongoing review

    We meet on a regular schedule to check results and plan what comes next.
